About Hire Shield

Our Mission & Vision

At Hire Shield, our mission is to provide fair, bias-free, and transparent campus hiring predictions using advanced AI and machine learning. We envision a future where every student has an equal shot at success with clear insights into their placement prospects.

Transparent bias detection helping to mitigate unfair hiring practices.

🎯

📂

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Hire Shield ensure unbiased hiring? ▼
Hire Shield applies advanced bias detection algorithms to analyze model fairness, helping recruiters make impartial decisions.
What data does Hire Shield use? ▼
Hire Shield uses academic records, aptitude scores, and resume details, processed securely to ensure privacy and accuracy.
How private is my data? ▼
Your data is never stored beyond analysis. Hire Shield follows strict privacy protocols to protect user information.
